## What You Need

Use a desktop browser and the Google account approved for the class. Confirm app and AI access beforehand. Edition, administrator controls and language can affect availability.

Download the supplied fictional practice pack. No real client or personnel record is needed. Dataset week fields are relative practice weeks, not an agreed pilot start date.

## How to Use the Exercises

Follow the numbered steps, attempt the output and then open the expected-result box. AI wording can vary; source facts and approval conditions must remain correct.

Values and prompts follow the notes. Replace source placeholders before submitting. Inspect proposed edits before applying them. Drafting does not authorise sending, inviting people or publishing.
