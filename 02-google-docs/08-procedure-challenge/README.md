# 08 - Complete the Procedure Challenge

In this chapter you will independently revise a section and demonstrate that its facts and navigation remain correct.

> **Prompts and values:** use the [copy-paste page](./prompts.md) for the exact values used below.

**Target allocation:** 45 minutes: 10 explanation/demonstration, 15 guided build and casework, 15 independent challenge, 5 verification and repair. The active portion is 35 minutes by design. These are unmeasured targets awaiting a learner pilot.

**Your result:** A revised procedure with tested navigation and a checked handover.

## 8.4 Complete an Unfamiliar Revision and Reader Test

1. Make a clearly named capstone copy and write a change-impact list before editing. Use the final procedure change brief on the card.

2. Design the new escalation subsection with actions for missing owner, ambiguous status and absent approval evidence. Include what the contributor records and what remains unresolved.

3. Add a proposed review checklist to the procedure and label it proposed practice. Do not invent a new company approval policy.

4. Integrate the new subsection into the heading hierarchy, contents and Quick Start navigation. Update any worked example affected by it.

5. Review the sponsor summary and decision questions for consistency with the new route. Preserve the original budget and date caveats.

6. Name a checked revision and export the complete participant handout. Inspect navigation and page layout after conversion.

7. Give a reviewer the document and an unfamiliar test case. Ask them to follow the procedure without extra spoken instructions and record their decision path.

## Independent Challenge

Close the earlier step lists for the first attempt. Use the case input and your own reasoning. If you need help, record the hint and repeat the affected check independently.

1. Use the reviewer’s hesitation or error to revise one instruction. If no hesitation occurs, challenge them with a second case containing two missing fields.

2. Produce a one-page handover with source link, current release, outstanding review questions and three acceptance-test results.

3. Demonstrate a complete path from opening link to exception handling to review record. Your explanation must show why the procedure does not authorise spending.

## Evidence to Hand Over

- Integrated three-case escalation route with working navigation.
- A reader test, an evidence-based repair or a tested second case, and fresh complete export.
- One-page handover that distinguishes maintained source, snapshot and open decisions.

Record the defect or uncertainty you investigated, the evidence used, the repair or reasoned no-change decision, and the final state. Restore temporary changes or keep them in a clearly named scenario copy.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| The capstone only adds a new heading | Check for executable actions, worked examples, tested links and an independent reader result. |
| An input or earlier result is missing | Reopen the input named in Before You Begin; do not substitute private company data |
| A menu or feature is unavailable | Confirm the account and the shared access notes; record the missing step and ask the trainer for its fallback |
| The result differs from the checkpoint | Compare the exact source range, wording and conditions before repeating the action |
