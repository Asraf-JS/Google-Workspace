Project Reporting Pilot
Purpose: test a consistent weekly project reporting process at Seri Maju Projects in Petaling Jaya.
Scope and Outputs: Planning, Procurement and Site Coordination will produce a project brief, task register, weekly status update and project hub. Live construction records, purchasing commitments and external publication are out of scope.
Roles: Aina Rahman is the project coordinator. Farid Hassan is the sponsor.
Schedule: four weeks from an agreed start date, with an update every Friday. Start date: To be confirmed.
Budget: proposed ceiling RM 12000, not approved.
Risk: teams may use different definitions of task status.
Success Measure: all three teams submit on time for the final two weeks.
Decisions Needed: sponsor approval of the budget and pilot start date.
