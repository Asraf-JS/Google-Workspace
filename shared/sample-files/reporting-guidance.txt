FICTIONAL TRAINING MATERIAL
Proposed reporting guidance for practice, version 1
This is proposed training guidance, not an approved company policy.
Not Started: work on the task has not begun.
In Progress: work has begun but the owner has not confirmed completion.
Complete: the task owner has confirmed that the agreed task output is ready for review.
DueWeek is a relative practice week from 1 to 4. It is not a confirmed calendar date.
Report the task ID, owner, status and a short exception note when needed.
If an owner, fact or decision is missing, flag it for clarification. Do not invent a value.
Prepare one weekly update for review every Friday during the pilot.
The source fact sheet controls project purpose, proposed budget and success measure.
This guidance does not approve the budget, the start date or any purchase.
