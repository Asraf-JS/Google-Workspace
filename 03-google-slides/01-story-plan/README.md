# 01 - Read the Brief and Plan the Story

In this chapter you will choose the audience, decision and evidence; use Gemini to propose an outline.

> **Prompts and values:** use the [copy-paste page](./prompts.md) for the exact values used below.

**Your result:** A six-slide story plan with a decision request and source evidence.

3. Choose which facts the audience must know before deciding.

## 1.4 Build an Audience and Evidence Storyboard

1. Create a planning Doc with columns Slide, Audience question, Main message, Evidence and Visual approach. Populate all six planned slides before opening the theme gallery.

2. Write the sponsor’s two unresolved decisions and the information needed for each. Separate a budget proposal from evidence of approval.

3. Create a claim inventory from the fact sheet and the 12-task workbook. Mark task status as a dataset snapshot, not evidence that the four-week pilot has succeeded.

4. Read the stakeholder requests on the card and classify each as supported, proposed or unsupported. Record the source phrase for each supported claim.

5. Draft a 60-second opening and a 30-second closing request. Keep the proposed budget and unresolved date in both where relevant.

6. Plan one appendix slide for source definitions and one audience question the evidence cannot answer. Explain why these are support material rather than part of the main story.

7. Walk a partner through the storyboard without slide decoration. Record where the decision logic is unclear and revise the sequence or message.

## Independent Challenge

Close the earlier step lists for the first attempt. Use the case input and your own reasoning. If you need help, record the hint and repeat the affected check independently.

1. Independently prepare a three-slide version for a sponsor with only two minutes available. Identify which evidence moves to notes or an appendix and why.

2. Compare the short and full outlines for all required facts. Restore any qualification lost through compression.

3. Write a response to the unsupported request on the card, including what evidence would be needed to make that claim.

## Evidence to Hand Over

- Six-slide evidence storyboard with two planned appendix topics.
- Opening, decision request and a justified three-slide alternative.
- Stakeholder-claim classification and one recorded narrative revision.

Record the defect or uncertainty you investigated, the evidence used, the repair or reasoned no-change decision, and the final state. Restore temporary changes or keep them in a clearly named scenario copy.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| A polished outline lacks a decision | Return to the sponsor’s open questions and identify which slide supplies evidence for each. |
| An input or earlier result is missing | Reopen the input named in Before You Begin; do not substitute private company data |
| A menu or feature is unavailable | Confirm the account and the shared access notes; record the missing step and ask the trainer for its fallback |
| The result differs from the checkpoint | Compare the exact source range, wording and conditions before repeating the action |

## Lesson Summary

The story plan gives the deck a purpose and an evidence base.

**Check yourself:** What decision should the sponsor understand at the end?
