## 8.4 Handle a Last-Minute Sponsor Change

1. Duplicate the checked baseline deck and record the source URL of each linked chart before editing the copy.

2. Read the final change brief and classify its requests as editorial changes, data changes or unsupported claims. Write an impact list.

3. Prepare a two-minute delivery version while preserving source caveats and a useful decision request. Move detail into notes or the appendix as appropriate.

4. On a separate data copy change T003 to Complete, refresh/reinsert the intended linked chart and update the narrative. Verify source identity before claiming the deck is current.

5. Audit the changed deck’s facts and scope. Do not change the budget or date merely because the change request asks for more confident wording.

6. Rehearse with one challenge question from a reviewer. Locate the supporting cell or source sentence instead of improvising a benefit claim.

7. Export a revised handout and compare it with the native deck. Label both as the changed scenario, leaving the original baseline intact.

## Independent Challenge

Close the earlier step lists for the first attempt. Use the case input and your own reasoning. If you need help, record the hint and repeat the affected check independently.

1. Deliver the two-minute changed-scenario update without the chapter instructions. Ask a reviewer to identify the requested decisions and the evidence population.

2. Use their feedback to make a targeted revision and repeat the critical part of the delivery.

3. Hand over a source map, revision manifest and open-question list. Explain how to restore the baseline and why a PDF does not restore itself.

## Evidence to Hand Over

- Change classification, impact list and two-minute revised narrative.
- Verified changed-scenario chart, source notes and matching handout.
- Reviewer result, targeted improvement and reproducible handover.

Record the defect or uncertainty you investigated, the evidence used, the repair or reasoned no-change decision, and the final state. Restore temporary changes or keep them in a clearly named scenario copy.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| The change request is treated as evidence for every claim | Separate the explicit task-status scenario from unsupported approval and outcome assertions. |
| An input or earlier result is missing | Reopen the input named in Before You Begin; do not substitute private company data |
| A menu or feature is unavailable | Confirm the account and the shared access notes; record the missing step and ask the trainer for its fallback |
| The result differs from the checkpoint | Compare the exact source range, wording and conditions before repeating the action |
