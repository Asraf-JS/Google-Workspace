# Lab Depth and Timing Pilot

## Why the Masterclass Changed

The original chapter allocations overstated the amount of work. A brief core path took approximately five minutes of automated operation, and several independent tasks were too small to justify a long exercise. Six larger assignments now combine connected skills, changed requirements, diagnosis and evidence-based verification. Revision 0.4 applies the depth revision to all 44 specialist chapters as well.

## Completion Standard

Every lab must produce a useful asset, require several connected skills, contain an independent change or fault, and leave evidence of a check and correction. A fast learner who finishes the guided path proceeds to the required challenge. An extension is optional and must not be used to conceal an undersized core lab.

The masterclass targets 60, 65, 55, 80, 60 and 70 minutes respectively. Their active portions are 42, 45, 38, 56, 42 and 50 minutes. These are planned allocations only. Reading, copying, loading or waiting for AI is not evidence of meaningful exercise depth.

## Run the Timing Pilot

Use at least three representative learners where practical. Record each learner separately; do not multiply automated timings to estimate a classroom duration. Keep the target account, feature route and prior experience with the results.

For each lab record: access/setup start and finish; demonstration; guided build; independent challenge; verification/repair; interruptions; fallback used; evidence complete; help required; and total active time. Pause for unrelated interruptions. Keep trainer explanation separate from learner work.

Use the blank pilot-log.csv in this folder. One row represents one learner and one lab. Use pseudonyms in public review material. A working file is not complete until the learner can explain the source, change and check.

If learners finish the complete meaningful core in under 30 minutes, review the task design and add a genuine analysis or repair requirement. Do not impose waiting or add redundant clicks. If the required core consistently exceeds the allocation, remove or move a topic and repilot. Examine the fastest and slowest complete attempts as well as the typical duration; a median alone can hide an unsuitable level.

Check the whole day for dependencies: T013 is introduced in Lab 4; its actual cost changes in Lab 5; Lab 6 changes and restores T003 while retaining T013. Missing outputs must have a stated recovery route.

## Evidence Status

Only the older brief creation, AI-recovery, heading and version core was executed previously. The expanded six-lab sequence, review/export work, independent challenges, resource-access tests and new timings remain unexecuted. The original capture is retained with that limitation. Screenshots must come from real successful application steps.

## Specialist Depth Revision

All 44 specialist chapters now include substantial casework, a changed requirement or fault, independent tasks and evidence gates. Their original guided path is only the starting point. Timetable allocations were rebalanced within 390 minutes per day, with 30 to 65 planned active minutes per specialist lab. No expanded route or allocation has yet been validated with learners. The [specialist depth map](./SPECIALIST-DEPTH-MAP.md) records the task and evidence for each chapter. Keep topics appropriate to the course level.
