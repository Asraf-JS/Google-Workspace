## Review protocol

Check source identity and authority; compare each consequential assertion with an exact passage; distinguish supported, contradicted and not established; retain known status separately from future unknown decisions; flag omissions separately from false claims; check personal/confidential input suitability and unresolved reuse rights; identify any proposed external action and the actual human authorisation required. Reject unsupported certainty without inventing its opposite. Do not infer capability or trustworthiness from a person’s name or role.
