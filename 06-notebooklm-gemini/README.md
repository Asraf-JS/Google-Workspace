**Your result:** A checked FAQ, source-supported briefing, audio overview and tested prompt playbook.

## Inputs and Checks

Each lab will include supplied fictional inputs, a named output, a checkable expected result, independent practice and troubleshooting. AI wording may vary; factual checks and assessment criteria will stay fixed.

## Delivery Notes

A source citation is a starting point for checking a claim, not proof that the claim is correct. The pilot may be a repeatable manual AI workflow; deploying scheduled automation is a separate extension. Prepare a fixed sample pack so the course does not depend on material from another course.

## Expanded Lab Standard

The original guided steps are a starting point. Each chapter now adds a substantive case, independent changed requirement, failure diagnosis and named evidence. Active work is designed for at least 30 minutes per lab; actual pacing is unmeasured. See the [depth and pilot plan](../planning/LAB-DEPTH-AND-PILOT.md). Keep the baseline and scenario copies distinct. The specialist courses use their own starter inputs and do not require outputs from the masterclass.
