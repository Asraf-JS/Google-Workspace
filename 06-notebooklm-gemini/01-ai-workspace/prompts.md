### Evidence boundary and acceptance rules

Only S1-S3 are original core evidence. The fact sheet and meeting record have no supplied version; guidance is version 1 and proposed. Review examples are critique material; AI drafts and automatic overviews are outputs, not original evidence. Change cards require separate contexts. No real contract or employee medical record is needed.

The task is a five-question coordinator FAQ and short briefing for later generation. Combine purpose/duration/teams in topic 1, preserving all six separate test questions. Resupply originals in a fresh context. Keep budget proposed/unapproved, start unagreed and actions in relative weeks. Do not invent ROI, outcomes, policy or purchasing approval.

After generation, inspect cited text and record Supported, Partial, Absent or Contradicted. Accept complete supported facts with explicit gaps; repair omitted topics, ambiguity or weak pointers; hold consequential unsupported claims, invented approval/date or contradiction. Before generation, keep Not run and Not assessed. Independent classification, two extra questions and reviewer agreement remain pending.
